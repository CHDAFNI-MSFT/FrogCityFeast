## Data collected by the app

Frog City Feast does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data.
The app has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking, social
features, purchases, or third-party data-collection SDKs. It does not request
access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, or other personal
device services.

Players may enter a local profile name. Profile names, scores, progress,
achievements, story clues, settings, and accessibility preferences are stored
only on the device inside the app's private storage area. This local
information is not sent to the developer or any third party.

## Device operations

The app and its Godot runtime use limited system operations for local save-file
management, elapsed-time measurement, and available disk-space checks. These
operations are declared in the iOS privacy manifest and are not used to track
users or collect information.

## Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on the device until the user deletes the app and its
app data through iPadOS. Offloading an app may preserve its documents, so use
the iPadOS delete action when complete removal is intended. Because the app has
no account or server, the developer has no remote profile or cloud data to
delete.

## Support communications

If a user chooses to contact support outside the app, the developer receives
the contact details and message the user voluntarily provides. That information
is used only to respond to the request, maintain necessary support records, and
meet legal obligations. Users should not send passwords, signing credentials,
financial information, or other sensitive data in a support request.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any
other users. It has no online communication, account creation, advertising, or
tracking. App Store availability remains subject to Apple's assigned age rating
and the device family's parental controls.

## Apple services

Apple may process App Store transactions, installation information, or
diagnostics under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. Those Apple services
are separate from data collection by Frog City Feast.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy
answers must be updated before the changed version is released. The effective
date above will identify the current version.
